A note before you begin

Welcome Home

Home was never meant to rest on one person's shoulders.

So often, one person becomes the keeper of every schedule, every appointment, every grocery list and birthday and permission slip and allergy — every small detail that keeps a family running. It is a quiet, invisible weight, and it rarely gets set down.

This almanac was made to change that. Think of it as your family's second brain — a place where everything has a home, so it doesn't all have to live in yours. Fill it in once, keep it current, and let it carry what you've been carrying alone.

Now the answer to a hundred daily questions can be the same gentle one: look in the book. A babysitter, a grandparent, a husband, even an older child can open these pages and confidently care for your home when you're not the one holding it all together.

Take it slowly. You don't have to fill every page today. Start with the profiles and the emergency pages, and let the rest fill in over cups of coffee. This is a living book — it grows with your family.
